Portions of the SSD1683 port descend from the board vendor's demo code for that
panel; see the "Provenance and licence" section of README.md.

SolarOS includes a pure C adaptation of the SSD1683 waveform builder from
https://github.com/bootdsc/Fastwave-Eink-Driver
commit c7d20eeefd0b0a5015b335a54bb9b63b37bca88f.
The adaptation adds argument validation and separates panel transport from
waveform construction. The Apache license is included as fastwave.txt.

SolarOS also adapts the short drive/settle phases for SSD1685 in
epd_ssd1685_waveform.c. This experimental adapter uses the SSD1685 mode-1
four-block/eight-group LUT layout, synthetic hold/white/black RAM masks,
and the panel's factory source and gate voltages.
