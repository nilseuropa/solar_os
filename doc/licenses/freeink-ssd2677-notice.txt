SSD2677 RAM LUT transport parameters and drive selectors were adapted from
Free-Ink/freeink-sdk f1e1d2be2594cc4750d4f9114d13c80845691775,
libs/display/FreeInkDisplay/src/lut/Ssd2677Luts.h and driver/Ssd2677Driver.cpp.
The reference tables describe the Onyx Picco SE0400NQW47 firmware, build 2912.
SolarOS calibrates its own bounded short pulses for Sticky V2, keeps the
panel's OTP analog settings, and reuses its existing shared pulse accounting.
Source: https://github.com/Free-Ink/freeink-sdk
