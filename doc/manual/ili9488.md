# ILI9488 SPI displays

`ili9488` drives 320x480 ILI9488 TFT panels, including the LCDWIKI MSP3520
screen, on ESP32 and ESP32-S3. The `full` flavor includes the driver; custom
flavors can enable the `ili9488` group. It can also be selected as a primary
display through a board manifest or expansion profile.

Use an existing named SPI bus and free GPIOs approved by the board. For example,
on ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8, `spi0` uses SCK GPIO12 and MOSI GPIO11. With LCD
CS on GPIO10, DC/RS on GPIO4, RESET on GPIO5, and LED on GPIO6:

```text
expansion attach ili9488 lcd0 spi=spi0 cs=gpio10 dc=gpio4 reset=gpio5 bl=gpio6 pwm=1
display test lcd0
expansion detach lcd0
```

The default view is landscape, 480x320. `rotation=0|1|2|3` selects portrait,
landscape, inverted portrait, or inverted landscape. Defaults are `rotation=1`,
`bgr=1`, `invert=0`, and `clock=20000` (20 MHz). `clock` accepts 100..40000 kHz;
select a rate suitable for the panel and wiring. `bgr=0` changes color order,
and `invert=1` enables display inversion.

`reset`, `bl`, and `power` are optional. Software reset always runs during
initialization. `active=0` selects an active-low backlight; `pwm=1` enables
brightness control through `bl`. Optional `power` controls an active-high supply
enable. If no backlight pin is supplied, power the module's backlight separately.

The display needs only SCK, MOSI, and its control pins. LCD SDO/MISO may be left
disconnected when display readback is not needed. Touch and SD are independent
devices with their own chip selects and require a bidirectional SPI connection.
The touch controller can be attached through [XPT2046 resistive touch](xpt2046.md).
The MSP3520 uses 3.3 V logic; its supply input supports 3.3 V or 5 V according to
the module's power configuration. See the [module documentation](https://www.lcdwiki.com/3.5inch_SPI_Module_ILI9488_SKU:MSP3520).

Text, indexed graphics, and RGB565 frames use the normal display service. The
driver expands RGB565 into the ILI9488's three-byte RGB666 SPI pixel format in
small DMA bands. It does not allocate a full RGB666 framebuffer. Other TFT
controllers retain their two-byte RGB565 transfers. Multiple TFT expansion
targets can coexist, with separate geometry, rotation, brightness, and lifetime.

## Quick reference

Use `expansion attach ili9488 NAME spi=BUS cs=PIN dc=PIN` to attach a screen,
`display test NAME` to draw a test pattern, and `expansion detach NAME` to
release it. Optional bindings include `reset`, `bl`, `power`, `active`, `pwm`,
`clock`, `rotation`, `bgr`, and `invert`. Defaults: landscape, 20 MHz, BGR,
and no inversion. LCD SDO is not needed for display writes.
