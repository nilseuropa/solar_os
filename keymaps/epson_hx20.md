## Epson HX-20 keyboard to TCA8418 map

CN2 is an FPC cable located at the bottom right corner of the keyboard.
Silk labels 20 as the closest pin to the Space key.

| 2 | KSC1 | R1 |

| 4 | KSC3 | R3 |
| 5 | KSC4 | R4 |
| 6 | KSC5 | R5 |
| 7 | KSC6 | R6 |
| 8 | KSC7 | R7 |
| 9 | KRTN9 | C9 |
| 10 | KRTN8 | C8 |
| 11 | KRTN7 | C7 |
| 12 | KRTN6 | C6 |
| 13 | KRTN5 | C5 |
| 14 | KRTN4 | C4 |
| 15 | KRTN3 | C3 |
| 16 | KRTN2 | C2 |
| 17 | KRTN1 | C1 |
| 18 | KRTN0 | C0 |
| 19 | Power switch | Unconnected |
| 20 | LCD BUSY/SO | Unconnected |
